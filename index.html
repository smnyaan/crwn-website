FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is CRWN free to use as a client?

Yes. Discovering styles, building your Hair Profile, and booking professionals is always free for clients.

How do I know a professional understands textured hair?

Every professional profile shows their specialties, portfolio, and Crown Ratings from real clients, so you can book with confidence.

Can professionals import their existing bookings?

Yes. Connect StyleSeat, Vagaro, or Instagram in minutes, or add services manually — no need to start from scratch.

Is my Hair Profile private?

Your Hair Profile is yours. It's only shared with professionals when you choose to book with them.
